| Diagnostic               | What it means                                                                                                                     | What to do                                                                                    |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `DA-GAM-CONFIG-RESOLVED` | Informational: the Google stream session uses the network code and asset key in the diagnostic.                                   | Check that they match your Google stream.                                                     |
| `DA-GAM-CONFIG-MISSING`  | The Break Manifest carries Google breaks but no usable Google configuration, or Google is turned off with `gam` `enabled: false`. | Check that the channel is connected to Google, or remove the opt-out.                         |
| `DA-GAM-SESSION-FAILED`  | The Google stream session could not be started.                                                                                   | On Web, load `ima3_dai.js` on the page. Otherwise, check the error message in the diagnostic. |

Breaks that fail in the backend, for example because Google could not decision them in time, end up in the `ERROR` state and never reach the SDK. See [Troubleshooting](../../integrations/google/pod-serving.mdx#troubleshooting) on the Google Pod Serving integration page for the possible reasons.
