Static assets work in every [layout](../../concepts/breaks.mdx#layouts):

| Layout          | Static asset                                                                  |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Single          | A video that replaces the content.                                            |
| Double box      | A video next to your content, with an image or video `companion` behind both. |
| L-shape ad      | A video, with an image or video `companion` that fills the L-shaped backdrop. |
| L-shape content | An image that fills the L-shaped backdrop while your content keeps playing.   |
| Overlay         | An image or video on top of your content.                                     |

An image stays on screen for the asset's `duration`, or for the break's `duration` when the asset has none.

Static assets can also be used for [pause ads](../../concepts/breaks.mdx#pause): an image, or a progressive MP4 video that plays muted, shown while the viewer is paused.
