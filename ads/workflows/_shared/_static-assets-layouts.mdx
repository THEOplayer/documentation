Static assets work in every [layout](../../concepts/breaks.mdx#layouts) and as [pause ads](../../concepts/breaks.mdx#pause):

| Layout                | Static asset                                                                             |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `single`              | A video that replaces the content.                                                       |
| `double`, `lshape_ad` | A video, with an image or video `companion` that fills the background.                   |
| `lshape_content`      | An image that fills the L-shaped backdrop while your content keeps playing.              |
| `overlay`             | An image or video on top of your content.                                                |
| Pause ad              | An image, or a progressive MP4 video that plays muted, shown while the viewer is paused. |

An image stays on screen for the asset's `duration`, or for the break's `duration` when the asset has none.
