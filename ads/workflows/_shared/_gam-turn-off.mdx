To keep Google out of a session — for example until the viewer gives consent, or in a build that must not contact Google — set `enabled` to `false` in the `gam` configuration. The SDK then skips the Google breaks in that session and reports `DA-GAM-CONFIG-MISSING`.

  container: document.getElementById('container'),
  gam: { enabled: false },
});
