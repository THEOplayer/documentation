VAST describes a linear (video) ad, so VAST assets work in the [layouts](../../concepts/breaks.mdx#layouts) where an ad video plays:

| Layout          | VAST asset                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Single          | Supported.                                                                                                           |
| Double box      | Supported. The VAST ad plays in the ad box, and the asset's `companion` fills the background.                        |
| L-shape ad      | Supported. The VAST ad plays in the ad window, and the asset's `companion` fills the L-shaped backdrop.              |
| L-shape content | Not supported. The SDK does not play the asset and reports `DA-VAST-UNSUPPORTED-FORMAT`. Use a static asset instead. |
| Overlay         | Not supported. The SDK does not play the asset and reports `DA-VAST-UNSUPPORTED-FORMAT`. Use a static asset instead. |

VAST assets can also be used for [pause ads](../../concepts/breaks.mdx#pause): the SDK reads the image from the VAST companion ads, or a progressive MP4 from the linear ad, and fires its impression tracking when shown.

VAST breaks play in server-guided (SGAI) sessions only.
