# Google Ad Manager

With Google Ad Manager 360, Google decides which ads fill each break. OptiView Ads announces the breaks to Google, Google assembles the ads into a pod, and the pod plays when the break starts. Use this workflow when you sell your inventory through Google Ad Manager 360, and to reach platforms that need server-side ad insertion.

Google Ad Manager is the ad source that supports both delivery modes of the [hybrid platform](./index.mdx#hybrid-ad-insertion-sgai-and-ssai):

|      | Server-guided (SGAI)                                                                                             | Server-side (SSAI)                                                                                    |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Live | [Google Pod Serving](../integrations/google/pod-serving.mdx): the SDK inserts Google's ad pods into your stream. | [Google DAI](../integrations/google/dai.mdx): Google stitches the ads into the stream for the player. |
| VOD  | Google pod serving for on-demand assets: the SDK inserts Google's ad pods at the break positions of the asset.   | Google DAI for on-demand assets: Google stitches the ads into the asset for the player.               |

Prefer SGAI on every platform that runs the OptiView Ads SDK: the media stream stays untouched, and the advanced [layouts](../concepts/breaks.mdx#layouts) such as double box and L-shape are available. Use SSAI for platforms where a client-side SDK is not an option, such as Roku and older connected TV devices. One channel can serve both, [per device](./index.mdx#hybrid-ad-insertion-sgai-and-ssai).

## Before you start

- The [Google Ad Manager prerequisites](../integrations/google/index.mdx) — network code and service account — are in place for your organization.
- Your channel is connected to Google: with the custom asset key of your Google stream for [Google Pod Serving](../integrations/google/pod-serving.mdx#configure-your-channel), and with the DAI asset key for [Google DAI](../integrations/google/dai.mdx#configure-your-channel).

## Live and VOD

### Live

- **SGAI** — OptiView Ads announces each break to Google ahead of its start, within the [look-forward time](../integrations/google/index.mdx#look-forward-time), so Google can decision the pod in time. Breaks can be scheduled, [punched](../concepts/breaks.mdx#break-punching), or created by [break detection](../concepts/marker-detection.mdx). See [Google Pod Serving](../integrations/google/pod-serving.mdx) for the Google Ad Manager setup and how to schedule a pod break.
- **SSAI** — the Dolby Ads Proxy inserts the break cues into the stream that Google DAI reads, and Google stitches the decisioned ads into the stream that viewers receive. See [Google DAI](../integrations/google/dai.mdx).
- Allow for the [decisioning margin](../integrations/google/index.mdx#decisioning-margin): a Google break must be created at least a few seconds before its start.

### VOD

- **SGAI** — schedule the Google breaks at their positions in the asset, in seconds, on a `mediatime` channel. Google decisions the pods when the viewer's session starts, and the SDK inserts them at the break positions.
- **SSAI** — Google stitches the ads for the asset's breaks into the on-demand stream, and the player plays the stitched asset.
- Because every viewer watches from their own position, ads are decisioned per viewer session rather than per break moment: there is no audience-wide peak at a break start.

Contact your Dolby OptiView account team to enable Google Ad Manager on your VOD channels.

## What your app needs

The SDK reads the Google settings — your network code and the channel's Google asset key — from the channel's Break Manifest, and starts the Google stream session automatically when the session starts. You do not configure them in the app, so your app and the backend always agree on which Google stream the ads belong to.

| Platform     | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Web          | Load Google's IMA DAI SDK on the page, before your application script (see below).                                                                                                                               |
| Android      | Nothing extra: the SDK runtime includes the Google IMA SDK.                                                                                                                                                      |
| iOS and tvOS | Nothing extra: the runtime pod includes the Google IMA SDK. With Swift Package Manager, add the Google IMA packages as described in [iOS](../player-integration/optiview-ads-sdk/ios.mdx#swift-package-manager). |
| React Native | Nothing extra: the native SDKs include the Google IMA SDK.                                                                                                                                                       |

On Web, add the IMA DAI loader to your page:

```html
<script src="https://imasdk.googleapis.com/js/sdkloader/ima3_dai.js"></script>
```

If your channel also uses [VAST](./vast.mdx) breaks, load `ima3.js` as well; the two scripts work side by side.

### Server-side sessions

When the channel steers a device to SSAI, the SDK requests the stitched stream from Google and loads it into your content player instead of your original stream URL. Your app keeps the same integration and starts the session with the same Break Manifest URL. On Web, use a content player adapter that exposes its video element, such as the HLS.js, Shaka, or native video adapter. In a server-side session, the SDK still plays the [event-triggered breaks](../concepts/breaks.mdx#event-based-triggers) — pre-roll, pause, and post-roll — on the device.

:::note[Upgrading from an earlier integration]
Earlier versions of this guide set a `networkCode` in the `gam` configuration and a `customAssetKey` in the session configuration. The SDK no longer accepts them: remove both, and the SDK takes the values from the Break Manifest.
:::

### Turn Google off for a session

To keep Google out of a session — for example until the viewer gives consent, or in a build that must not contact Google — set `enabled` to `false` in the `gam` configuration. The SDK then skips the Google breaks in that session and reports `DA-GAM-CONFIG-MISSING`.

## Ad targeting parameters

For Google, `assetParameters` are sent as Google [ad tag parameters](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/10678356) (`adTagParameters`). As a rule of thumb:

- Set the values that describe your content on the break, such as `description_url` and `plcmt`. See [Required Google parameters](../integrations/google/pod-serving.mdx#required-google-parameters).
- Set the values that depend on the playback environment in the SDK, such as `vpa` and `vpmute` and, in apps, the [device and app identity parameters](../integrations/google/pod-serving.mdx#additional-parameters-for-app-inventory).

When the same key is set in both places, the value on the break wins. See [Ad targeting parameters](../how-to-guides/ad-tag-parameters.mdx) for the merge rules and platform examples.

## Troubleshooting

| Diagnostic               | What it means                                                                                                                     | What to do                                                                                    |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `DA-GAM-CONFIG-RESOLVED` | Informational: the Google stream session uses the network code and custom asset key in the diagnostic.                            | Check that they match your Google live stream.                                                |
| `DA-GAM-CONFIG-MISSING`  | The Break Manifest carries Google breaks but no usable Google configuration, or Google is turned off with `gam` `enabled: false`. | Check that the channel is configured for Google Pod Serving, or remove the opt-out.           |
| `DA-GAM-SESSION-FAILED`  | The Google stream session could not be started.                                                                                   | On Web, load `ima3_dai.js` on the page. Otherwise, check the error message in the diagnostic. |

Breaks that fail in the backend, for example because Google could not decision them in time, end up in the `ERROR` state and never reach the SDK. See [Troubleshooting](../integrations/google/pod-serving.mdx#troubleshooting) on the Google Pod Serving integration page.
