# OptiView Player

:::info Legacy integration
OptiView Player is the legacy integration path for OptiView Ads. For any new integration, we recommend the [OptiView Ads SDK](../optiview-ads-sdk/index.mdx).
:::

The OptiView Player SDKs ship with a built-in OptiView Ads integration that handles the [Break Manifest](../../concepts/break-manifest.mdx) logic, the Google IMA/DAI integrations, and OptiView Ads impressions.

OptiView Player **version 11.9.x or later** is required.

## Supported layouts

OptiView Player supports all break [layouts](../../concepts/breaks.mdx#layouts):

- **Single:** one ad covering the full player surface.
- **Double Box:** the ad and a companion asset side by side.
- **L-Shape Ad:** the ad shrinks into a corner with a companion asset filling the backdrop.
- **L-Shape Content:** your content keeps playing in a corner while the ad fills the backdrop.
- **Overlay:** the ad renders on top of your content while it keeps playing.
