# Frame accurate seeking

This example shows how to step through a video frame by frame, or jump directly to a specific frame.

A frame number is converted to a time by dividing it by the frame rate of the video (24 frames per second for this video),
and the player seeks to the middle of that frame by setting `player.currentTime`.
The current frame number is calculated the other way around, from `player.currentTime`.
