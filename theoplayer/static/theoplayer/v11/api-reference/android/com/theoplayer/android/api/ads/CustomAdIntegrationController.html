Package com.theoplayer.android.api.ads

Interface CustomAdIntegrationController

  • All Implemented Interfaces:
    @ApiStatus.Experimental() 
public interface CustomAdIntegrationController

    The common controller for a custom ad integration.

    Use this from your handler to update the integration's ad state, report playback progress, and report errors.

      • reportAdClick

         abstract Unit reportAdClick(Ad ad)

        Report that the user clicked on the given ad's click-through, or on one of its companions.

        • The player will fire an AdClickedEvent on every call.

        • The player does not open the click-through (LinearAd.getClickThrough, NonLinearAd.getClickThrough or CompanionAd.getClickThrough); the integration is responsible for that.

        • The ad does not need to be playing, since a companion can stay visible after its ad ended.

        Parameters:
        ad - The ad that was clicked.

      • removeAd

         abstract Unit removeAd(Ad ad)

        Remove the given ad.

        • The ad will be removed from Ads.getCurrentAds and Ads.getScheduledAds.

        • If the ad was currently playing, it will first be ended.

      • endAdBreak

         abstract Unit endAdBreak(AdBreak adBreak)

        Ends an ad break reported by the integration.

        An ad break begins implicitly when its first ad is started.

        • Any ads that are still playing are ended first.

        • An AdBreakEndEvent and an AdBreakChangeEvent are fired.

        • The break remains in adBreaks, and ads that have not started remain in Ads.getScheduledAds.

        • Call removeAdBreak to remove the break afterwards.

        • If the break is not currently playing, this does nothing.

        Parameters:
        adBreak - The ad break to end.

      • getContent

         abstract ReadonlyContentPlayer getContent()

        Read-only access to content playback.

        For server-side integrations, this reflects the stitched stream, including ads. For client-side integrations, it reflects content only and is not affected by linear ads from this or another integration. During linear ad playback, content remains paused at the position where it was interrupted.
