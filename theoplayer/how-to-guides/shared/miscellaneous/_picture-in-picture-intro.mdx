The Picture-in-Picture (PiP) feature allows you to create a floating player.
This is commonly used to let the video remain playing on screen even if:

- The user scrolls to another section of the page (causing the original player to no longer be visible).
- The user sends the application to the background.
- The user opens a new page within the same application.

THEOplayer distinguishes two flavors of Picture-in-Picture:

- With **in-app** Picture-in-Picture, the visibility of the PiP window is contained to the inside of the app.
  In other words, it goes to background and foreground together with the application.
- With **out-of-app** Picture-in-Picture, the visibility of the PiP window is not contained to the inside of the app.
  It can remain visible while the user navigates to other views, pages or apps.

Picture-in-Picture is a presentation mode of the player.
As such, you can listen for a `presentationmodechange` event, or read the player's presentation mode,
to find out what the presentation mode is at a given moment or to detect a change.
