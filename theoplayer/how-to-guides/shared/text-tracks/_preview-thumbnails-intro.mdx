This article describes how you can enable preview thumbnails, which THEOplayer implements through the TextTrack API.
The thumbnails are loaded as a separate text track, by side-loading them as a text track in your source description.

A demo of the preview thumbnails on the THEOplayer Web SDK is available at [https://demo.theoplayer.com/preview-thumbnails](https://demo.theoplayer.com/preview-thumbnails).
